How We Work
Our job is not simply to deliver professional learning. We know our teachers and their goals, prepare intentionally, move implementation forward, and close the loop.

I Move It

I Close the Loop

Our Approach
Prisms coaching builds teachers' capacity to independently implement the Prisms Lesson Arc and five Core Instructional Routines, so students develop each module's core conceptual understanding.
Every coaching visit has four elements
  • Coaching focus — the area of implementation this visit addresses
  • Teacher growth goal — what the teacher will do more effectively or independently
  • Evidence of progress — what the teacher says, plans, or does, plus student evidence
  • Next step — what the teacher implements next, with the preparation and follow-up needed
The Lesson Arc
Frame~2–3 min→VR~20–25 min→Synthesis~10 min→Debrief~5 min
Proximal and End-of-Unit CFUs are required. Transfer Day is optional.
